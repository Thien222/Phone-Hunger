# Audio inventory

`app/src/main/assets/audio/rumble.wav`, `chew.wav`, and `burp.wav` are original procedural waveforms generated for this repository on 26 November 2026 using Python standard-library synthesis. They contain no third-party samples, voice recordings, or music. The project owner may distribute and modify them with PhoneHunger.

English and Vietnamese spoken lines are generated at runtime by the device's installed offline Android TTS engine. They are not bundled voice assets. Users can record/import their own files; those files stay in app-private storage. The owner must review the terms of any TTS engine used to distribute synthesized recordings outside the user's device.
